Campus and Learning Environment

Colorado Springs

AUHT is located on North Academy Boulevard in Colorado Springs, Colorado. The city is known for its natural beauty, outdoor lifestyle, healthy living culture, education resources, and growing technology sector. With the Rocky Mountains and surrounding natural areas nearby, Colorado Springs offers strong quality of life and a healthy-city atmosphere.

AUHT's campus and administrative spaces are designed to integrate teaching, online program operations, academic conferences, research project management, student services, international cooperation, and health-technology demonstration into a modern learning environment. The university also supports remote students, working professionals, and international learners through digital learning platforms.
