ACADEMICS

Academic Structure

From foundational professional skills, to graduate-level specialization, to advanced post-graduate practice and postdoctoral research — AUHT offers a multi-tier system of health-technology education and research.

Four Tiers

A Pathway for Every Stage

AUHT's academic system serves students entering the field as well as established professionals with bachelor's, graduate, or doctoral backgrounds.

① Foundational Professional Certificate

2-Year Program

For students, working professionals, and career changers entering healthcare technology, public health, health data, behavioral medicine, or integrated care. Four semesters across two years, balancing theory and practice.

View Programs

② Graduate Certificate

1-Year Program · 18 Credits

For applicants with a bachelor's or equivalent background. One year, six core courses, blending academic theory, technical skill, case study, data analysis, project design, and a Capstone applied project.

View Programs

③ Post-graduate Certificate

2-Year Program

For clinicians, healthcare administrators, public-health professionals, health-tech specialists, and policy analysts with a master's or equivalent background. Emphasizes advanced theory, real cases, policy perspective, and research translation.

View Programs

④ Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Research Training

For doctorate-holders pursuing research in healthcare, public health, lifestyle medicine, health data, health policy, value evaluation, sustainable wellbeing, and interdisciplinary health science.

Teaching Model

How We Teach

AUHT uses flexible, multimodal, application-oriented teaching tailored to each program's level and goals.

Multimodal Delivery

Online learning, hybrid teaching, case discussions, data labs, skill workshops, project-based learning, research-report supervision, Capstone projects, and simulated practice.

Tangible Outcomes

Students produce data reports, policy memos, health-communication plans, community assessments, dashboards, evaluation plans, research reports, and Capstone deliverables.

Comprehensive Support

Academic advising, writing and methods coaching, career guidance, international student support, online learning tools, integrity training, and portfolio development.
