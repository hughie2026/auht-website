Academic Programs

Interdisciplinary academic pathways for healthcare technology careers.

AUHT offers foundational professional certificates, graduate certificates, post-graduate certificates, and advanced research development pathways.

Program Structure

Built for applied learning and professional growth.

AUHT’s academic model is designed for learners entering the health technology field, professionals seeking specialization, and advanced scholars pursuing applied research development.

Two-Year Foundational Professional Certificates

For students, working professionals, and career-transition learners building professional foundations in healthcare technology and public health.

One-Year Graduate Certificates

For learners with undergraduate-level preparation who seek advanced skills in biomedical informatics, epidemiology, or healthy city science.

Post-Graduate Certificates

For clinicians, managers, public health practitioners, policy analysts, and health industry leaders pursuing advanced professional development.

Graduate Certificates

One-year specialized programs.

Connects healthcare, life sciences, data science, information systems, electronic health records, interoperability, analytics, AI, and clinical decision support.

Epidemiology

Develops competencies in epidemiological principles, research design, causal inference, surveillance, outbreak investigation, R-based analysis, and public health communication.

EPI 530 Field Epidemiology
EPI 590 Applied Epidemiology Capstone

Healthy City Science

Explores the relationship between cities and health through urban planning, GIS, built environment, climate resilience, health equity, and smart city governance.

HCS 501 Foundations of Healthy City Science
HCS 530 Urban Health Data and GIS
HCS 540 Climate and Health Resilience
HCS 590 Healthy City Action Lab
Post-Graduate Certificates

Advanced professional development.

Population Health Sciences

Focuses on population health data science, AI, real-time surveillance, precision public health, social determinants, health equity, learning health systems, and implementation science.

Health Policy and Management

Covers health policy analysis, governance, health law, healthcare strategy, finance, economics, value-based care, quality, safety, digital operations, and transformational leadership.

Some professional fields may involve government regulation, licensure, clinical qualifications, or industry certification requirements. AUHT programs should not be understood as automatically granting regulated professional licenses.
