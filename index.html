About AUHT

A University Built for the Future of Health

The world today faces aging populations, rising chronic disease, public-health risks, digital transformation, AI in medicine, health inequities, and the challenge of sustainable wellbeing. Single-discipline training alone is no longer enough.

AUHT integrates medical science, public health, data science, AI, behavioral science, policy, and sustainability — preparing applied, research-oriented, and leadership-capable professionals to solve real-world health problems and advance more equitable, effective, and human-centered care systems.

Core Values

What We Stand For

Five values guide our teaching, research, and community engagement.

Health for All

Health is shaped by individuals, families, communities, cities, policy, and technology working together.

Technology with Humanity

AI, data science, and digital tools must serve human dignity, patient safety, equity, and social wellbeing.

Evidence into Practice

We prepare students to design programs, analyze data, evaluate outcomes, and drive real improvement.

Ethics and Responsibility

Research ethics, privacy, academic integrity, and professional boundaries are foundational, not optional.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Medicine, nursing, public health, data, management, behavior, urban studies, law, and sustainability working together.
