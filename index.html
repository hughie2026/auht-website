Research and Innovation

A university research platform for practical health challenges.

AUHT’s research ecosystem supports interdisciplinary work across computational health, applied epidemiology, lifestyle medicine, whole-person health, health policy, ethics, and sustainable wellbeing.

Computational Health

Clinical data, AI, decision support, health analytics, real-world evidence, patient safety, and digital health evaluation.

Applied Epidemiology

Disease prevention, surveillance, chronic disease management, health behavior, program evaluation, and community health.

Sustainable Wellbeing

Health equity, social determinants, policy, healthy cities, health communication, ethics, and human-centered service innovation.

Learning that produces real outcomes.

AUHT courses are designed around applied projects, case-based learning, data laboratories, ethics training, research supervision, and professional portfolio development.

  • Case seminars and professional simulations
  • Health data analysis and digital health projects
  • Community assessment and public health planning
  • Research reports, Capstone projects, and policy briefs

Inspired by Colorado Springs.

AUHT is connected to a city known for its natural environment, outdoor lifestyle, educational resources, and growing innovation culture. The university’s learning model supports both local engagement and global participation.
