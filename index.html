About AUHT

A future-oriented university for healthcare technology education.

American University of Healthcare Technology prepares applied, research-oriented, and leadership-oriented professionals for modern healthcare systems and global public health challenges.

Mission

To cultivate professionals capable of improving individual health, population health, healthcare service quality, and social wellbeing through application-oriented and interdisciplinary education.

Vision

To become an internationally influential university of health technology with strengths in health data, public health, behavioral medicine, health policy, healthy cities, and sustainable wellbeing.

Core Values

  • Health for All
  • Technology with Humanity
  • Evidence into Practice
  • Ethics and Responsibility
  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Academics

Certificate programs designed for health technology careers.

AUHT offers foundational professional certificates, graduate certificates, post-graduate certificates, and postdoctoral-level research scholar pathways.

Healthcare Data Science

Focuses on healthcare systems, Python, SQL, statistics, data visualization, machine learning fundamentals, privacy, governance, and responsible AI.

Public Health

Covers epidemiology, biostatistics, health policy, community assessment, health communication, emergency preparedness, GIS, and global health.

Behavioral Medicine

Integrates health psychology, behavior change, chronic disease support, health coaching, stress management, sleep health, and ethical boundaries.

Biomedical Informatics

Includes EHR systems, clinical data, FHIR interoperability, databases, analytics, AI, and clinical decision support.

Epidemiology

Develops competencies in epidemiological principles, study design, R-based analysis, surveillance, outbreak investigation, and causal inference.

Healthy City Science

Connects urban health, health equity, GIS, built environment, climate resilience, smart city governance, and healthy city action.

Population Health Sciences

Focuses on population health data science, AI, precision public health, social determinants, health equity, implementation science, and value-based care.

Health Policy and Management

Covers health policy analysis, governance, health law, healthcare strategy, finance, economics, digital operations, quality, safety, and leadership.

Research Center

Interdisciplinary research for health systems and public wellbeing.

AUHT’s University Research Center supports postdoctoral-level research training, health data research, applied epidemiology, lifestyle medicine, whole-person health, ethics, and compliance.

Institute for Healthcare Big Data and Computational Health

Research areas include clinical big data, AI-assisted diagnosis, clinical decision support, prediction models, smart nursing, patient safety, imaging, pathology, natural language processing, and digital health evaluation.

Institute for Applied Epidemiology and Lifestyle Medicine

Focuses on disease prevention, chronic disease management, health behavior, lifestyle interventions, population health, community health, and implementation science.

Institute for Whole-Person Health and Sustainable Wellbeing

Studies health equity, social determinants, wellbeing, healthcare value, medical humanities, health communication, environment, cities, and social innovation.

Ethics Committee

Provides ethics review, compliance guidance, privacy support, academic integrity oversight, and guidance for human subject and health data research.

Leadership

President’s Profile

Prof. Dr. Jonathan Whitaker serves as President of American University of Healthcare Technology. He promotes the university’s strategic development, academic planning, international cooperation, educational quality, and research platform development.

AUHT emphasizes academic rigor, professional practicality, ethical responsibility, and a global public health perspective.
Admissions

Programs for students, professionals, researchers, and global learners.

AUHT welcomes learners seeking careers in health technology, public health, biomedical informatics, epidemiology, behavioral medicine, policy, and research.

Foundational Applicants

For students and career-transition learners interested in entering healthcare, public health, health data, behavioral health, and health technology fields.

Graduate Applicants

For individuals with undergraduate-level backgrounds seeking professional advancement in biomedical informatics, epidemiology, or healthy city science.

Post-graduate Applicants

For learners with advanced academic or professional backgrounds in healthcare, policy, management, population health, digital health, or public health leadership.

Internationalization

Building global partnerships in health technology and public health.

AUHT develops international cooperation in online education, public health data, postdoctoral-level research supervision, health technology projects, healthy cities, health equity, seminars, conferences, and academic publishing.

Strategic Cooperation

AUHT has signed a comprehensive strategic partnership agreement with the Hong Kong Public Health Technology Research Center and plans to establish a China Project Office.

Collaboration Areas

  • International online course collaboration
  • Public health and health data projects
  • Healthcare quality improvement research
  • Healthy city and health equity research
  • Academic conferences, seminars, and workshops
Legal and Compliance

Professional boundaries and academic responsibility.

Certificate programs do not automatically grant physician licensure, nursing licensure, psychotherapy qualifications, clinical practice authority, immigration benefits, employment guarantees, or regulated professional licenses. Students should confirm qualification requirements according to the laws of their country, state, or region.
