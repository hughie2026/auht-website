Our Mission

Education. Research. Clinical Impact.

AUHT is built on three integrated commitments — to train rigorous health professionals, to advance translational science, and to deliver care that improves outcomes across populations.

— 01 EDUCATION

Training tomorrow's health leaders.

Programs in health data science, public health, biomedical informatics, epidemiology, and population health — taught through case-based, evidence-driven curricula and supervised research.

Academic Programs
— 02 RESEARCH

Translational discovery.

From clinical AI and real-world evidence to applied epidemiology and behavioral medicine, AUHT investigators pursue research that moves from data to bedside to community.

Research Centers
— 03 CLINICAL IMPACT

Care, equity, and wellbeing.

Through partnerships, training sites, and digital health initiatives, AUHT advances safer, more equitable, and more sustainable care for individuals and populations.
