Global Partnerships

AUHT builds its health-technology platform with a global outlook, welcoming partners across countries and regions.

AUHT welcomes students, scholars, healthcare professionals, public-health practitioners, health-tech companies, research institutions, community organizations, and policy bodies from around the world. Through international cooperation, we aim to advance the exchange of knowledge and the development of talent in healthcare technology, public health, digital health, population health, and sustainable wellbeing.

Strategic Partner

Featured Partnership

Hong Kong Centre for Public Health Technology Research

Strategic Partnership · Asia Office Plan

AUHT has signed a comprehensive strategic partnership agreement with the Hong Kong Centre for Public Health Technology Research, with plans to establish a China program office. This partnership strengthens AUHT's exchange with the Asia region in public health, health technology, and talent development.

Cooperation Areas

Eight Directions for Collaboration

AUHT actively pursues collaboration across the following directions and welcomes partner institutions worldwide.

International Online Courses

Joint online programs with overseas institutions to expand the reach of health-technology education.

Public Health & Health Data Projects

Collaborative public-health program design, health-data analysis, and surveillance research.

Postdoctoral Research Mentoring

Cross-institutional and cross-border supervision of postdoctoral research fellows.

Health-Tech Industry Projects

Applied projects and joint talent development with health-technology companies.

Quality Improvement Research

Partnerships with hospitals and community institutions for quality improvement and translational research.

Urban Health & Equity Research

Cross-regional research on urban health, health equity, and sustainable wellbeing.

Conferences & Workshops

Joint conferences, professional symposia, and interdisciplinary workshops.

Academic Publishing

Joint advancement of academic publishing, research translation, and knowledge dissemination.
