Admissions

Begin your academic journey in health technology and public health.

AUHT welcomes motivated learners from diverse academic and professional backgrounds who are interested in healthcare technology, data science, public health, policy, research, and human wellbeing.

Who Should Apply

Programs for different stages of professional development.

Foundational Learners

Suitable for students, early-career learners, and career-transition professionals who wish to enter healthcare, public health, health data, or behavioral health fields.

Graduate-Level Learners

Suitable for applicants with undergraduate-level preparation who want to develop specialized competencies in informatics, epidemiology, healthy cities, or related fields.

Advanced Professionals

Suitable for clinicians, managers, researchers, policy professionals, and industry leaders seeking advanced development in population health, policy, and management.

Application Process

A clear pathway from inquiry to enrollment.

Applicants are encouraged to review program expectations carefully and prepare academic and professional materials that demonstrate their readiness and goals.

Step 1

Choose a Program

Review academic pathways and identify the program aligned with your goals.

Step 2

Prepare Materials

Prepare academic background information, résumé or CV, statement of purpose, and other requested documents.

Step 3

Admissions Review

The admissions office reviews applicant background, goals, and program fit.

Step 4

Enrollment Guidance

Admitted students receive program guidance, learning platform information, and academic advising.

Important Notice

Professional boundaries and compliance.

Certificate programs do not automatically grant physician licensure, nursing licensure, psychotherapy qualifications, clinical practice authority, immigration benefits, employment guarantees, or regulated professional licenses. Applicants should confirm qualification requirements according to the laws of the country, state, or region where they intend to study or work.
