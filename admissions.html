Admissions

AUHT's tiered programs serve a wide range of applicants — from those entering the field, to senior researchers extending their doctoral careers.

Who Should Apply

Post-graduate Certificate

For applicants with a master's or equivalent background, advancing in health policy, healthcare management, population health, value-based care, digital health, or public-health leadership.

View Programs

Postdoctoral Research Fellow

For doctorate-holders pursuing research in healthcare, public health, lifestyle medicine, health data, health policy, value evaluation, sustainable wellbeing, and interdisciplinary health science.

How to Apply

Application Process

① Inquire

Reach out at consult@auht.us.org to discuss the program that fits your background and goals.

② Apply

Submit your application form, academic credentials, personal statement, and supporting materials to admission@auht.us.org.

③ Review

The admissions committee reviews your background, materials, and program fit comprehensively.

④ Admit

Upon admission, complete registration, tuition, and onboarding per the university's written instructions.

Career Outlook

Where Graduates Go

AUHT programs serve fast-growing fields across healthcare technology, public health, health data, behavioral medicine, health policy, population health, urban health, and healthcare management.

Healthcare data analytics · Clinical data coordination · Public-health program support · Community health services · Health education and promotion · Disease surveillance and public-health data analysis · Patient engagement and chronic-disease management · Health coaching support · Healthcare quality improvement · Health-tech product and operations · Biomedical informatics · EHR and interoperability analysis · Epidemiology analysis · Urban health and GIS analysis · Health policy analysis · Healthcare management and value-based care · Population health management · Health equity and social determinants research · Digital health and AI governance support.

Important Notice

Some career fields involve government regulation, professional licensure, clinical credentialing, or industry certification. AUHT coursework should not be interpreted as automatically conferring licensure as a physician, nurse, psychotherapist, public-health practitioner; nor as immigration eligibility, employment guarantee, or any regulated professional license. Applicants are responsible for confirming the qualification requirements applicable in their country, state, or region.

Legal and Compliance Statement

AUHT certificates, research training, and academic mentoring programs are governed by officially published program documents, admission letters, tuition policies, completion standards, and written agreements. Unless otherwise expressly stated in writing, certificate programs do not automatically confer any regulated professional license, clinical credential, government qualification, immigration benefit, employment guarantee, or recognition by any third-party body.

The remote Postdoctoral Research Fellow program is a self-funded, remote, non-employment, non-degree, non-credit, non-visa research-training and academic-mentoring program. It does not constitute U.S. employment and does not provide salary, employee benefits, work authorization, or immigration sponsorship. Whether a paper is published or accepted by any specific journal depends on research quality, author contribution, journal scope, peer review, editorial decision, and publication processes; the university makes no guarantee of acceptance or publication by any third-party journal.
