Post-graduate Certificate Programs

A two-year program for clinicians, healthcare administrators, public-health professionals, health-tech specialists, policy analysts, and senior health-industry leaders.

The Post-graduate Certificate emphasizes advanced theory, real cases, data-driven analysis, policy perspective, managerial decision-making, and research translation. Each certificate consists of three advanced courses and a research-report capstone. Under faculty supervision, students complete an applied research project of practical value — a deliverable that can inform institutional management, public-health decision-making, policy, health-tech innovation, or system improvement.

1. Post-graduate Certificate in Population Health Sciences

Population Health · Equity · Implementation Science

This program centers on population health, data science, equity, precision public health, and learning health systems — preparing senior professionals to use data, AI, implementation science, and policy thinking to address complex health problems.

Program Structure

  • PHSC 701 — Advanced Population Health Data Science, AI, and Real-Time Surveillance
  • PHSC 702 — Precision Public Health, Social Determinants, and Health Equity
  • PHSC 703 — Learning Health Systems, Implementation Science, and Value-Based Population Health Care
  • PHSC 790 — Population Health Sciences Research Report

Who Should Apply: Professionals in clinical care, public health, health data, healthcare management, payers and insurance, community health, and policy.

Sample Research Topics: AI risk stratification, chronic-disease management, equity, community health, maternal health, climate and health, value-based care, public-health intervention evaluation.

2. Post-graduate Certificate in Health Policy and Management

Policy · Strategy · Quality · Digital Transformation

This program addresses health policy, system governance, healthcare management, payment reform, quality and safety, and digital transformation — preparing senior leaders with policy judgment, managerial decision-making, financial analytic ability, and organizational leadership.

Program Structure

  • HPM 701 — Advanced Health Policy Analysis, Governance, and Health Regulation
  • HPM 702 — Healthcare Strategy, Finance, Economics, and Value-Based Management
  • HPM 703 — Digital Health Operations, Quality and Safety, and Transformation Leadership
  • HPM 790 — Health Policy and Management Research Report

Who Should Apply: Healthcare administrators, public-health and policy professionals, health-tech executives, payer and insurance professionals, clinical-administrative leaders, and learners moving into health management and policy.

Postdoctoral

Postdoctoral Research Fellow Program

For doctorate-holders pursuing research in healthcare, public health, lifestyle medicine, health data, health policy, value evaluation, sustainable wellbeing, and interdisciplinary health science.

Important Program Notice

The remote Postdoctoral Research Fellow program is a self-funded, remote, non-employment, non-degree, non-credit, non-visa research-training and academic-mentoring program. It does not constitute U.S. employment and does not provide salary, employee benefits, work authorization, or immigration sponsorship. Whether a paper is published or accepted by any specific journal depends on research quality, author contribution, journal scope, peer review, editorial decision, and publication processes; the university makes no guarantee of acceptance or publication by any third-party journal.
