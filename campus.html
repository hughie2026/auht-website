Campus and Learning

A modern learning environment inspired by Colorado Springs.

AUHT’s learning environment is designed to support academic study, digital learning, research supervision, professional development, and international academic exchange.

Colorado Springs

AUHT is located in Colorado Springs, Colorado, near the eastern foothills of the Rocky Mountains. The city is known for its natural landscape, outdoor lifestyle, health culture, and growing innovation environment.
