Internationalization

Building global cooperation in health technology, public health, and research.

AUHT welcomes cooperation with universities, research centers, healthcare institutions, public health organizations, technology companies, and professional communities.

Global Strategy

Connecting education, research, and health innovation.

AUHT’s international cooperation model is designed to support online education, academic exchange, applied research, health data projects, public health practice, and professional development.

Strategic Cooperation

AUHT has established a comprehensive strategic partnership with the Hong Kong Public Health Technology Research Center and plans to develop a China Project Office to support regional cooperation, academic exchange, and project development.
